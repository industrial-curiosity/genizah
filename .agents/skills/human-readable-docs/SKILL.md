# Human-readable documentation

Write for a reader who has only this document.

- Use clear, precise terms. Define a term when its meaning is not evident from context.
- State requirements and facts directly. Do not rely on implication, vague qualifiers, or ambiguous pronouns.
- Use the fewest words that preserve the full meaning. Remove repetition, filler, and details that do not help the reader act or understand.
